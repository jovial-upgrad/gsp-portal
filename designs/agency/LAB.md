# Finance lab (branch `finance-lab`)

Jovial, 19 Nov: a separate instance for working out University terms from the real contracts, without changing the
live prototype. This branch is never merged into `main`; it is published as its own artifact.

- Roles: RP Admin and GSP · Finance only (`LAB_ROLES` in `src/proto/roles.ts`).
- GSP · Finance's menu: Home, Applications, Course search, Recruitment, Master and Institution partners, Universities,
  Courses, Commission lines and Setup (no Inbox, My work or Students).
- Contract analysis: the Sheet "University Contract Clauses" (extraction, gaps, contracts).
