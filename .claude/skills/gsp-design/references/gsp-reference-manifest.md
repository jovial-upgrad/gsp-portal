# GSP reference manifest (48 images)

The 48 images the GSP team gathered for GSP 3.0, bundled in `assets/gsp-references/`. Each entry says what the image shows, what is worth taking from it, and which GSP screen it helps. Open the image with `view` before borrowing from it; the notes are a guide, the picture is the reference.

Most images are concept shots in device mockups (tilted monitors, tablets, phones in hand). Read them for structure, components and moments, not exact specs. Several share a family look (applyr, Finsera / Loanza / Lendora, Salesforce concepts), noted below.

## Contents

1. Study-abroad edtech (applyr): 06, 14, 16
2. Finance, lending and accounting dashboards: 01, 17, 18, 22, 28, 29, 32, 42, 48
